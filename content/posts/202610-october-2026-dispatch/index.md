Welcome back to **Dispatch**, a personal roundup of videos, records, and anything that I found interesting this month. 
Think of **Dispatch** as my monthly mixtape from the internet with no real theme except “this was neat.” 
## Videos
- Key by Cars & Bids spent [72 Hours in South Dakota](https://youtu.be/c68ZlDd3n50) the incredible car culture of the place with some cool cars and picturesque roads. If you couldn't be bothered with the entire video then skip to [34:16](https://youtu.be/c68ZlDd3n50?t=2056) cause Tanner is bad ass and inspirational, PLEASE just watch that part 😂. 
- Human skill genuinely never ceases to amaze me and that was the case watching this [delivery of a 500-tonne transformer](https://youtu.be/O5juKwZ3kjY) in a convoy through very tight roads. It's a SLOW 20-minute video that surprisingly hooks you in but that's perhaps the ETS2 enthusiast in me speaking. 
- Once in a while you get a new channel posting an amazing first video such as Jesse Magal's [case study on Lodwar town in northern Kenya](https://youtu.be/RkcMva3cRmQ). He "compares the Lodwar of ten years ago with the busy town you see today." That single video has me keen on how he improves in the coming videos.  
## Music
-   [Nothing But Thieves - Crazy In Love (Beyoncé cover)](https://youtu.be/gR41QL4k8uE) - NBT has quickly become one of my best discoveries this year and this cover proves why I love this band. 

And here are some albums I enjoyed:
- [This Music May Contain Hope by RAYE](https://album.link/tmmch) - almost every day I have a new favourite off of the album and I think that's the hallmarks of a great album. It also helps that it's unique and playful with its theatrics. 
- [Sweet Fortune by Ryan Beatty](https://album.link/sweet-fortune) - if you loved Calico then Ryan somehow manages to follow up on that with a joyful twist. 
- [Nothing But Thieves (Deluxe)](https://album.link/nbt) - this self-titled album is so beautiful. Conor Mason reminds me of Jeff Buckley in the very best way. 
## Films & Shows
Films:
- [The Furious (2025)](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/1280738) - if you love action films then you'll definitely love this. It's action action action. We follow Wang Wei who goes against a criminal network without the help of the police in his bid to rescue his kidnapped daughter. He and a journalist deliver relentless martial arts action full of fury couple with great cinematography.
- [Beautiful Thing (1996)](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/10938-beautiful-thing) - this is such a beautiful and tender coming-of-age story with an amazing film look. I could watch it purely for the film visuals but its more than just that. Also, it's very British.

Shows:
- [Spider-Noir (2026)](https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/220102-spider-noir) - first of all, impeccable casting with amazing acting from Nicolas Cage and the entire team. Also, this is not your typical Spider-Man story, we follow Ben Reilly, an aging private investigator, who is  torn between his superhero past and his current hands off lifestyle. I didn't watch the black and white version but I hear the vibes might be better. 
- [Lanterns (2026)](https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/95350-lanterns) - i don't even know how to describe this, just check out the trailer, otherwise this is the overview: "Two intergalactic cops, new recruit John Stewart and Lantern legend Hal Jordan, are drawn into a dark, Earth-based mystery as they investigate a murder in the American heartland."
## Books
After quite a couple of slow reading months, I finally finished _The Odyssey_ by Homer (translated by Robert Fagles). 
The book totally lived up to the hype. 
I surprisingly loved its heavy reliance on flashback in the form of Odysseus telling the story of his journey. 
By the end of the book, I found myself more and more interested in greek mythology especially having loved _The Song of Achilles_ by Madeline Miller a few years back. 
My initial goal was to finish this before the Christopher Nolan film dropped but I clearly failed at that. 
Nonetheless, I totally can't wait to finally watch the adaptation. 
## Podcast Episodes
- [Gobi](https://thisislovepodcast.com/episode-24-gobi) - this is such a wholesome story of a man attempting one of the toughest races in the world with the goal to win but all that changes when a furry little creature joins him. Dion and the little dog embark on journey together that basically changes their lives. 
- [Jimmy Carr on What Now? with Trevor Noah](https://youtu.be/ssOaEejtEwM) - this is one of the most intellectual discussion I've heard recently which is not surprising being a podcast from Trevor Noah. It also helps that Jimmy Carr is funny, disgustingly well read and very quoteable.
